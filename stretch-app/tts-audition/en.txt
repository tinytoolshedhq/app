# sit_side_reach
Let your left hand slide down your side, and lean just a little to the left.
Nice and slow, come back to the middle.
Now slide your right hand down, and ease over to the right.
Back to center. Sit tall and relax.

# stand_calf_wall
Take a small step back with your left foot. Keep the heel down and the front knee soft.
Slowly bring it back in and stand steady.
Now step your right foot back, heel down, and ease into the stretch.
Bring your right foot in, and stand nice and easy.

# lie_single_knee_hug
Hold behind your left thigh and gently bring that knee toward you.
Slowly lower your left foot back to the floor.
Switch to your right thigh and bring the knee in. No need to pull hard.
Lower your right foot and take a breather.

# pronunciation_test
Feel a gentle stretch through your calf and hamstring, never forcing it.
Circle your ankles slowly, and let your lower back and quadriceps relax.
