English

Thank you for using Decibel Meter.

Tap Start and allow the microphone. The on-screen number is an estimate, for reference only. In Settings you can add or subtract up to 20 dB.

Full Pack is a one-time App Store purchase of US$2.99. No subscription, ads, or trial.

Free

  • Estimated current, max, average, and min
  • Level explanation (for example ~60 dB ≈ normal conversation)
  • One calibration
  • Sessions up to 5 minutes
  • Up to 10 records, view-only

Full Pack

  • Unlimited records
  • Export CSV, image, and PDF
  • Unlimited measurement duration
  • Multiple named calibrations
  • Vibration alert over a threshold you set

To restore a purchase, go to Settings > Restore Purchases in the app.

Readings are estimates for reference only.
